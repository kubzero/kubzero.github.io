BackNest: USB Photo Backup

Copy photos and videos from your iPhone to USB flash drives, portable drives, and SD cards. No cloud upload — files stay on your drive.

Why USB backup

  • Own your data — save originals to removable storage you control
  • Works with any folder on a connected drive
  • Resume after USB disconnect or when you return to the app
  • Optional SHA-256 verification while copying
  • Restore from USB back to Photos (Pro)

How it works

  1. Connect your USB drive or SD card
  2. Allow Photos access
  3. Choose a folder on your drive
  4. Start backup and keep the app open while copying

Files are saved in a clear “media backup” folder on your drive, ready to use on a computer or another device. Originals in Photos are not deleted during backup.

Free to try

  • Back up 20 photos at no cost
  • Resume interrupted photo backups within your free limit

USB Backup Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Unlimited photo backup
  • Video backup
  • Restore from USB to Photos
  • Family Sharing eligible (when enabled on the purchase)

Important

  • A USB-C drive or adapter is required on most iPhones
  • Keep the app open during copying — iOS may pause transfers in the background
  • Full Photos access is recommended for a complete library backup

Perfect for travel, extra storage, switching phones, or keeping a private offline copy of your memories.
