Proofacto

Compose job documentation on your iPhone. Photos, notes, on-device acknowledgements, and PDF export — private on your device, no cloud sync.

Why Proofacto

  • Capture site photos and notes in one work document
  • Optional sketches and on-device acknowledgements
  • Export clean PDF packages for clients or records
  • Private by design — documents stay on your iPhone

How it works

  1. Create a work document for the job or site
  2. Add photos from Camera or Photos, plus notes
  3. Optionally sketch or collect acknowledgements
  4. Export a PDF and share it your way

Everything runs on your iPhone. ByteGlo does not sync your documents to our servers. ByteGlo is the software provider only — not a proof or dispute service.

Frequently asked questions

Can I create PDF job reports on my iPhone?

Yes. Proofacto combines job photos, notes, optional sketches, and on-device acknowledgements into documents you can export as PDF and share yourself.

Are my work documents stored on ByteGlo servers?

No. Proofacto is offline-first and does not sync your work documents to ByteGlo servers. You control the PDFs you export and share. Uninstalling removes local app data.

Does a Proofacto report certify legal evidence?

No. Proofacto is document-composing software, not a legal certification, notarization, or evidence service. You are responsible for checking the report and how it is used.
