Proofacto

Document the job on your iPhone. Photos, notes, signatures, and PDF export — private on your device, no cloud sync.

Why Proofacto

  • Capture site photos and notes in one work document
  • Optional sketches and party signatures on device
  • Export clean PDF packages for clients or records
  • Private by design — documents stay on your iPhone

How it works

  1. Create a work document for the job or site
  2. Add photos from Camera or Photos, plus notes
  3. Optionally sketch or collect signatures
  4. Export a PDF and share it your way

Everything runs on your iPhone. ByteGlo does not sync your documents to our servers.

Free to try

  • Create work documents and capture photos
  • Limited free PDF exports with watermark

Premium (one-time purchase)

  • Unlimited PDF exports without watermark
  • Full templates and documentation features
  • One-time unlock — no subscription

Important

  • No cloud sync — work documents stay on this iPhone
  • PDFs are documentation helpers, not legal certification
  • Uninstalling removes local app data (backups you made separately may remain)

Built for trades and field work — document once, share as PDF, keep control on your device.
