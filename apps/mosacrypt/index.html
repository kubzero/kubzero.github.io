MosaCrypt

Seal any photo with a password, share the encrypted PNG like a regular image, and reveal it privately on your device. No accounts, uploads, tracking, or cloud storage.

Coming soon

Why MosaCrypt

  • Authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption using Apple CryptoKit
  • Ciphertext is carried inside lossless PNG pixels for portable sharing
  • Encryption, key derivation, and reveal happen on your device
  • A private Sealed Library keeps local copies ready to share or reveal

How it works

  1. Take a photo or choose one you have allowed MosaCrypt to access
  2. Choose a password and seal the photo locally
  3. Share or save the lossless MosaCrypt PNG
  4. Open it later and enter the same password to restore the original

Your password is used to derive the encryption key and is not stored by ByteGlo. Keep it safe: neither MosaCrypt nor ByteGlo can recover a forgotten password.

Free to try

  • Three total seal, reveal, or browse-unlock uses
  • Optimized photo resolution and the standard MosaCrypt watermark

Premium (one-time purchase)

  • Unlimited sealing, revealing, and browsing
  • Original-resolution sealing
  • Turn the watermark off or customize its text, symbol, and color
  • Pay once — no subscription

Important

  • Keep the password: encrypted photos cannot be recovered without it
  • Keep the PNG lossless — editing, conversion, or messaging-service compression may damage its encrypted pixels
  • Replacing a Photos item creates a new item; the previous item may remain in Recently Deleted and iCloud may retain copies
  • Sealed Library copies are local and are removed when you uninstall the app; exported copies remain where you saved them

Built for private, portable photo protection — local encryption, lossless sharing, and no ByteGlo cloud account.
