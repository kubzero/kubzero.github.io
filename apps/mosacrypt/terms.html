3. What MosaCrypt does

MosaCrypt is an on-device utility that encrypts image data using a password-derived key, packages the encrypted payload into lossless PNG pixels, and attempts to reveal valid MosaCrypt images when the correct password is supplied. It can also keep local copies in a Sealed Library and interact with Photos when you grant permission.
