GiftBell

Never miss a birthday. Gentle reminders, home screen widgets, and optional on-device wish ideas — all private on your iPhone.

Why GiftBell

  • Local notifications so you never miss a birthday
  • Upcoming list and home screen widgets at a glance
  • Optional on-device AI for birthday wishes and gift ideas
  • Private by design — contacts stay on your device, no cloud sync

How it works

  1. Add contacts or import birthdays from Contacts
  2. See who’s next in Upcoming and on widgets
  3. Get local reminders when the day arrives
  4. Optionally download an on-device model for wish and gift ideas

Reminders work without the AI model. Wish generation runs on your iPhone after an optional ~2.3 GB download — your contact data is not uploaded for ideas.

Free to try

  • Up to 6 birthday contacts
  • Reminders, widgets, and limited wish/gift ideas and Contacts import

Premium (one-time purchase)

  • Unlimited birthday contacts
  • Unlimited wish and gift ideas
  • Unlimited Contacts import
  • One-time unlock — no subscription

Important

  • No cloud sync — birthday data stays on this iPhone
  • Optional AI model (~2.3 GB from Hugging Face); generation stays on device
  • JSON backup you control; uninstalling removes local data

Perfect for remembering the people who matter — privately, on your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

Do birthday reminders need an AI model?

No. Birthday reminders and widgets work without the optional AI model. Wish and gift-idea generation runs on your iPhone after an optional model download of about 2.3 GB.

Are my contacts uploaded for AI birthday wishes?

No. Wish generation runs on your device. GiftBell does not upload your contacts for ideas or sync birthday data to a ByteGlo cloud account.

Is GiftBell a subscription?

No. The free tier supports up to six birthday contacts and limited features. Premium is a one-time unlock for unlimited birthday contacts, wish and gift ideas, and Contacts import.
