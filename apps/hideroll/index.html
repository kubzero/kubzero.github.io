HideRoll: Private Photo Cleaner

Scan your Photos library on your iPhone. Flagged items stay covered until you preview — then move to the Hidden album or delete in one tap. Local AI only — no cloud upload.

Why HideRoll

  • On-device Core ML scan — photos and videos never leave your iPhone
  • Flagged items stay covered until you choose to preview
  • Move matches to the system Hidden album or delete from Photos
  • Adjustable detection level, optional App Lock with Face ID

How it works

  1. Allow Photos access (full library recommended)
  2. Scan photos and videos with the bundled on-device model
  3. Review covered findings in Found Media
  4. Hide in Photos or delete — you confirm every action

Classification runs only on your device. ByteGlo does not upload, store, or receive your photo library. Media that is not available locally (for example iCloud-only originals) may be skipped.

Free to try

  • Scan up to 300 photos and 10 videos
  • Preview up to 3 found items

Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Unlimited photo and video scans
  • Preview all found media
  • Hide and delete findings
  • App Lock with Face ID / Touch ID

Important

  • On-device helper only — results can include false positives; you review before acting
  • Delete from Photos may be permanent — confirm carefully
  • No cloud sync or ByteGlo accounts — settings and findings stay on this device
  • Uninstalling removes local app data

Built for private library cleanup — scan on device, keep findings covered, act when you are ready.
