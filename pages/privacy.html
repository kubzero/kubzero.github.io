1. Introduction

ByteGlo OÜ (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we process personal data when you visit this website, and your rights under applicable law.

3. What we collect

We do not maintain a user database, accounts, or mailing lists on this website.

  • Email. If you write to us, we keep your message and email address only to reply and, if needed, to follow up on that inquiry.
  • Hosting logs. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses in standard server logs to operate and secure the service. We do not use that data to identify you or to track browsing habits.
  • Language preference. If you choose English or Estonian, we store that choice in your browser’s local storage so the site can open in the same language next time. That value is not sent to us as analytics.

4. Legal basis

We process personal data on the basis of our legitimate interest in responding to inquiries, running a secure website, and remembering your language choice on your device, and to meet legal obligations where they apply.

5. Cookies and tracking

We do not use tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising pixels, or similar third-party trackers. We do not show a cookie consent banner because we do not set tracking cookies.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information. Hosting infrastructure may process technical logs as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention

Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle the inquiry, or longer if the law requires. Hosting logs are retained by the provider for a limited operational period. Language preference stays on your device until you clear site data.

8. Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have rights under the GDPR, including access, rectification, and erasure, subject to legal limits.

Because this website has no accounts and no tracking profiles, we generally do not hold personal data about a visitor unless you have emailed us. Access or erasure requests therefore relate to that correspondence. Contact .
