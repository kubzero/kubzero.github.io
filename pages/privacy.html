3. What we collect

We do not maintain a user database, accounts, or mailing lists on this website.

We do not use tracking cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, advertising pixels, or similar third-party trackers. We do not show a cookie consent banner because we do not set tracking cookies.

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal identification information. We may disclose information if required by law.

Any service provider that processes personal data for us is required to protect that data with safeguards no less protective than those described in this policy, consistent with Apple App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1 and applicable law.

We do not share website visitor data with advertising networks or analytics SDKs, because we do not use them on this site.
